Privacy PolicyArrow Puzzle: Dot Art Reveal

◷   Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Welcome to Arrow Puzzle: Dot Art Reveal. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent gaming experience. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information and data.

By installing and playing Arrow Puzzle: Dot Art Reveal, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

01Information Collection and Use

A. Personal Data

Arrow Puzzle: Dot Art Reveal does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, financial details, or precise GPS location. You do not need to create an account or sign in to play the game.

B. Game Progress & Local Storage

All your gameplay data—including:

  • Completed puzzle levels and unlocked stages
  • Star counts and high scores
  • Daily streak counters
  • Game settings (sound effects, background music, haptic vibration preferences)

are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's private internal storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

02Device Permissions Used

The application requests only minimal, essential permissions strictly needed for gameplay and advertising:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to fetch and display advertisements served by Google AdMob.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check whether an active internet connection is available before requesting advertisements.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to provide optional haptic tactile feedback when tapping arrows during puzzle gameplay. No vibration data is collected or tracked.

03Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Arrow Puzzle: Dot Art Reveal free for all players, we display third-party advertisements using Google AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC).

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal device information, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Coarse IP address (used for approximate geographic region / country-level ad delivery)
  • Device model, operating system version, and screen resolution
  • Ad interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, video completion for rewarded ads)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic performance metrics

This data is used solely to deliver contextual or personalized ads, prevent ad fraud, ensure frequency capping, and analyze ad performance in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

You can learn more about how Google handles your data and privacy by reviewing their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob & Privacy: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Personalized Ads Opt-Out: You can opt-out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID).

04Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Arrow Puzzle: Dot Art Reveal is designed as a family-friendly casual puzzle game for general audiences of all ages.

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to third parties through our game, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt action to resolve it.

05Data Retention and Deletion

Because all game progress is stored entirely on your local device:

  • You have complete control over your data.
  • You can reset or delete all game progress and preferences at any time by clearing the application's storage in your device settings (Settings > Apps > Arrow Puzzle > Storage > Clear Data) or by simply uninstalling the application.

06Security

We value your trust in playing our game. Since we do not transmit personal data over the internet or maintain remote user databases, your game progress remains secure within the isolated application sandbox of your Android operating system.

07Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, app features, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates.
